eight scroll-craft questions were not asked. Answers below are either the
user's words (quoted) or authored decisions (marked *authored*).

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** From the reference video: a quiet, overcast garden, dark foliage, still
   water, a camera that walks in. *Authored:* calm, tactile, grown, precise.
2. **Journey.** "step into a garden": opening frame with the brand, then the
   walk across the pond and up the stepping stones, arriving at the building.
3. **Energy.** *Authored:* calm start, steady middle, the arrival is the loudest
   moment because the building fills the frame and the copy changes.
4. **Feeling.** *Authored:* see curve below. The one moment: arriving at the house.
5. **Only-this-site move.** *Authored:* every step you take lands on the water
   and sends a ripple across the pond.
6. **Range.** Keep the existing Patina & Messing identity (colours, Montserrat,
   leaf logo). Premium-minimal, as the current site.
7. **One world or scenes.** One world: "step into a garden". This is a single
   hero scene, not a whole page.
8. **Assets.** The reference video (1280x720, brand overlays not ours), the
   logo SVGs, Montserrat. The scene is rebuilt from the video's opening frame.

## Hard constraints

- Castle and tree on **separate layers**, so a real castle image can replace
  the placeholder pavilion later.
- Brand: `#1F3A36` Patina, `#A8B0A4` Salbei, `#B99A63` Messing, `#E8E3D9`
  Kalkstein, `#292B2A` Graphit. Montserrat Bold and Regular only. Leaf logo.
- Copy in first person, plain German, no em dashes (site copy rules).
- No invented facts: copy is taken from the current site.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Invitation | Logo and claim over a still, dark garden; the water moves a little |
| Walk | Curiosity, then calm | The pond slides under you, each step ripples, reeds and boulder part to the sides |
| Arrival (peak) | "I am standing there" | The house fills the frame, the path ends at the terrace, the offer appears |
| Contact | Resolution | Limestone sheet with phone, mail, address |

**Peak:** arrival at the house. Largest span of the hero (about 40% of it).

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where you literally walk into the
garden, and every step ripples the pond."

**Authored silence:** the walk between the first copy leaving and the second
line arriving is intentionally copy-free.
